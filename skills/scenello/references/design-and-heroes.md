# Design language and hero composition

The inspected design-vocabulary clip provides visual categories, not a universal page template. Its artworks remain private. The separately mentioned hero-layout recording is unavailable at its historical path: this composition matrix is an independent design tool, not a transcription of unseen footage.

“Bauhemian” may mean Bauhaus or Bohemian. Do not conflate them; use the reference/context or clarify when the distinction determines the brief.

| Language | Structural decisions | Useful motion / fit | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bauhaus | Geometric forms, deliberate primary/neutral roles, functional type/grid | Objects, systems, geometric assembly | Random primary-colour shapes |
| Bohemian | Natural material, warm variation, relaxed editorial rhythm | Craft/travel; organic parallax | Clutter and unreadable textures |
| Brutalism | Exposed structure, assertive type, direct labels | Culture/experimental work; spatial reconfiguration | Breaking usability for roughness |
| Swiss | Clear grid, disciplined type, hierarchy and whitespace | Expertise/catalogues; precise card handoff | Sterile repetition unrelated to content |
| Human in action | Real gesture as focal evidence, generous movement space | Dance/sport/craft; articulated motion | False anatomy and static poses sold as choreography |
| Editorial luxury | Excellent material/light, measured type and negative space | Objects/travel; controlled approach/reveal | Generic black/gold and slogans |
| Minimal | Few exact relationships and a strong subject | Restrained meaningful transformation | Empty content or missing actions |
| Typographic | Type leads scale and composition | Publishing/culture; text into content | Gratuitous letter effects |
| Grunge | Controlled abrasion, collage and irregular rhythm | Culture; texture with legible controls | Noise or unlicensed textures |
| Vintage | Period-specific type/material/imagery | Heritage collections; tactile movement | Mixing unrelated eras |
| Abstract / kinetic | An original formal system and rhythm | Sound/art/tools; evolving geometry | Random particles without destination |
| Surreal | One consistent impossible premise with credible light/scale | Expressive transformations | Arbitrary object mixtures |
| Psychedelic / Y2K / cyberpunk | Choose one actual vocabulary and hierarchy | Relevant cultural contexts; optical change | Default neon/chrome on every project |
| Glass / material | Depth, surface and background relationships | Transparency that improves hierarchy | Large blur surfaces or generic frosted cards |

Carry the chosen language through navigation, hero, collection, detail, forms and footer. A complementary design must share type, colour roles, spacing or material logic. Do not style only the opening.

| Hero composition | Choose when | Motion and mobile adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| Central object | One product carries the story | Keep its anchor; reduce peripheral layers and reserve text space |
| Editorial split | Person/object needs space beside a statement | Reframe vertically without losing the movement |
| Full-bleed environment | Location establishes meaning | Author camera/occlusion and a useful bypass |
| Human in action | Movement is the brand's evidence | Frame reach, clothing and gesture; controls outside limb paths |
| Asymmetric poster | Subject supports expressive scale/tension | Preserve imbalance with clear reading/touch zones |
| Typographic lead | Language/expertise is strongest | Let text introduce a real object/card |
| Portal / shared element | Hero must become the next section | Preserve one geometry/anchor; next cards already move into view |
| Group / constellation | Collection/disciplines are central | Lead item, diagonal group turn, continuous individual handoff |

Compare two or three combinations against the subject, real content, asset rights, narrow-screen legibility and cost. Select one and implement. This is a decision system, not a mandatory theme-selector UI.
