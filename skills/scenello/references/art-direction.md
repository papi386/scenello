# Art direction as an implementation decision

A reference is useful when you can name its mechanism. Record the inspected URL/date, subject, composition, trigger, temporal sequence, mobile treatment, rights and uncertainty. A search snippet is a lead, not a visual review. If a page blocks access, mark it blocked; do not invent a viewing.

Write three short directions for substantial new work. Compare their emotional meaning, content hierarchy, credible asset route, engineering cost and narrow-screen composition. Choose one; do not blend unrelated movements because they are fashionable. Preserve an accepted direction during refinements.

## A practical visual contract

Describe: “The focal object begins at …; the visitor …; the object …; the next section inherits …; the user can …”. Specify the functional destination. One strong visual premise beats effects in every corner.

Pick typography through subject and reading needs. Test long real titles, numbers and non-English text before committing. Use an intentional display/body relationship and legible measures rather than arbitrary font variety. Fonts need their own licenses; a CSS CDN URL is not redistribution permission. System fonts are valid when selected deliberately.

Choose color roles (canvas, primary text, secondary text, active control, object, atmospheric light), not a list of trendy hex values. Check contrast on the actual composited background. An accent should communicate hierarchy or material, not decorate every heading.

## Three different routes, not house templates

* Optical research: depth, spectral light and generous black space; circular geometry expresses observation. Risks: illegible dim controls, oversized glow surfaces. Original demo: `assets/examples/observatory/`.
* Material archive: crisp paper, cobalt registration marks, tactile swatches and editorial numbering; motion expresses sorting. Risks: cards obscuring the catalogue or looking like a slideshow. Original demo: `assets/examples/materials/`.
* Human craft: natural side-light and a close physical gesture; use licensed photography, a controlled render or an honestly symbolic illustration. Risks: broken anatomy and mismatched perspectives. Do not pretend an SVG icon meets a photorealistic hand brief.

## Remove generic output by editing decisions

Audit duplicated cards, gratuitous labels, decorative stats, unnecessary buttons, forced hyphenated copy and meaningless dashboards. Keep structural devices that explain real structure. No style is inherently forbidden: brutalism, Bauhaus, editorial modernism and minimalism only work when their hierarchy and content support the subject. Adapt methods, never another site's proprietary layout, branding or assets.
