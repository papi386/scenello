# Material origin becomes the product

Independent technical reconstruction from a locally inspected reference. Source media, platform identities, brands and frame captures remain private. Approximate times describe the recording, which may start mid-page or loop; they are not a specification of source code or easing.

## Creative objective
Make a product feel rooted in its material through a meaningful transformation.

## Visual description
A dark product composition leads to a floating rough material, scattered fragments and a composed product scene. Large editorial typography shares the stage with the object.

## Temporal sequence and key moments
0–3s: product hero; 3–5s: isolated rough material; 5–8s: larger title and fragment field; 8–11s: material/product narrative; final seconds repeat earlier portions.

## Motion and camera
Object scale, surrounding fragments and light create depth. The source does not establish a true topology morph, procedural fracture or real-time simulation.

## Implementation
Choose between compatible authored morph targets, an aligned render sequence or an original layered dissolve. Do not stretch a bitmap stone into an anatomically unrelated product. Match silhouette, centre of mass and shadow at the crossover. Let a few purposeful fragments inherit velocity instead of random particle noise.

## Interaction
Scroll owns the material-to-product reveal. Keep the product call to action and descriptive information usable before and after the sequence.

## Responsive adaptation
Mobile reduces fragment count and reserves clear space around the product title. Crop scenery, never the essential silhouette.

## Accessibility
Reduced motion presents material and product side by side with concise provenance copy. Do not imply environmental claims unsupported by product evidence.

## Performance
Fracture geometry, transparent particle overdraw and high-resolution textures need limits. Prefer a few instanced pieces and compositor-friendly layers.

## Validation
Check silhouette transitions, material consistency, shadow grounding, word wrapping, asset failure and actual product navigation.
