# Observed mechanisms, independently reconstructed

Read only the mechanism relevant to the brief. These guides cover locally inspected website-reference recordings; none embeds source media, identities or branding. Source timestamps describe a recorded session, not proof of implementation. Original diagrams live inside the skill assets directory.

| Guide | Creative purpose |
|---|---|
| [Terrain passage and route continuity](terrain-passage.md) | Let a landscape become a navigable journey rather than a decorative background. |
| [Anchored service demonstration](anchored-restoration.md) | Explain a real service through changes to one stable subject, then lead into a functional enquiry path. |
| [Design vocabulary as a selection tool](design-vocabulary.md) | Choose a visual language for the product rather than copying a style label. |
| [Object inspection through camera scale](vessel-inspection.md) | Let a premium object reveal its function through coherent changes in viewing scale. |
| [Showroom corridor into a catalogue](garage-corridor.md) | Connect an immersive environment to an ordinary selectable inventory. |
| [Synchronized product and surface identity](surface-identity.md) | Use a product variant change to reorganize the whole visual world without losing the product identity. |
| [Material origin becomes the product](material-to-product.md) | Make a product feel rooted in its material through a meaningful transformation. |
| [Figure to garment and collection](figure-to-garment.md) | Let a selected item emerge from a wider collection through controlled fragmentation and scale. |
| [Botanical product constellation](botanical-product.md) | Give a product collection a spatial identity through ingredients that participate in its transitions. |
| [Exploded ingredients into preparation](exploded-ingredients.md) | Connect appetite, ingredients and preparation through an energetic but readable physical sequence. |
| [Continuous city descent into vehicle follow](city-to-vehicle.md) | Convert a city-scale establishing view into a close moving subject without a disconnected scene cut. |
| [Editorial image selector and spatial card world](editorial-card-world.md) | Use one coherent image world to move from attention to selectable content, rather than repeating the same project gallery twice. |
