# Exploded ingredients into preparation

Independent technical reconstruction from a locally inspected reference. Source media, platform identities, brands and frame captures remain private. Approximate times describe the recording, which may start mid-page or loop; they are not a specification of source code or easing.

## Creative objective
Connect appetite, ingredients and preparation through an energetic but readable physical sequence.

## Visual description
A food site includes a menu, close cooking vessel, ingredients/preparation stages, ordering controls and an opening food arrangement that breaks into a suspended ingredient field.

## Temporal sequence and key moments
0–3s: menu/content; 3–5s: close cooking surface; 5–8s: ingredient and preparation panels; 8–10s: ordering region; 10–13s: hero returns and food separates; 13–16s: suspended ingredients and sauce-like motion fill the composition.

## Motion and camera
The preparation close-up and suspended hero use different scales. The recording does not reveal physical simulation, original trigger logic or whether each apparent layer is live.

## Implementation
Use a single material/light rig or registered high-quality plates. For a throw, animate a clear vertical launch with controlled lateral drift; rotate local ingredient axes separately from world translation. Maintain plausible mass ordering, collision clearances and landing shadows. A readable fast flip can occupy a short scroll segment; do not speed the entire scene to hide poor timing.

## Interaction
Scroll can drive launch, suspended composition and assembly. For a brief fast flip, trigger a bounded short timeline once on entering its segment, then settle deterministically on reverse/jump. Keep menu selections and ordering controls real DOM; a visible form in a clip is not proof of a working backend.

## Responsive adaptation
Mobile reduces the ingredient field while keeping the assembled food large and text clear. Preserve complete flips within the viewport.

## Accessibility
Reduced motion presents assembled food, ingredient descriptions and the ordinary menu immediately. Avoid intense flashes or mandatory motion before ordering.

## Performance
Food realism depends on texture, light and geometry, not extra particles. Limit alpha overdraw, dynamic shadows and sequence download sizes; keep heavy render tools optional.

## Validation
Check gravity arcs, local rotation, ingredient thickness, shadow grounding, fast scroll, reverse/jump, semantic menu and unavailable checkout handling.
