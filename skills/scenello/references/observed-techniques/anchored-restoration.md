# Anchored service demonstration

Independent technical reconstruction from a locally inspected reference. Source media, platform identities, brands and frame captures remain private. Approximate times describe the recording, which may start mid-page or loop; they are not a specification of source code or easing.

## Creative objective
Explain a real service through changes to one stable subject, then lead into a functional enquiry path.

## Visual description
A house appears with changing decorative treatments. The sequence moves through editorial service content, an enquiry form and a daylight-to-evening house view with multiple object scales.

## Temporal sequence and key moments
0–4s: house/treatment variations; roughly 4–10s: service and quote sections; 10–17s: daylight house details; 17–21s: close decorative objects and night lighting; final seconds return to the first composition.

## Motion and camera
Wide and close views establish the house as the common world. Sparse recorded frames do not prove physically rigged workers or a continuous single camera.

## Implementation
Keep wall/window geometry registered between before/after layers. Tool contact, worker reach and revealed treatment must share one coordinate frame. A squeegee reveals a narrow trailing clean region; a roller reveals an overlapping textured stroke. Never animate the glass itself or reveal rectangular paint tiles unrelated to the tool.

## Interaction
Scroll maps to progress along authored tool paths and then a pullback. Ladders and worker limbs need credible scale, planted feet and supported contact; use a close tool-only view if a believable worker rig is unavailable. Enquiry controls remain actual form elements.

## Responsive adaptation
Reframe around one reachable service area on mobile, then expand toward the house silhouette. Keep descriptions beside or below the work area, not over it.

## Accessibility
Reduced motion presents matched before/after states with an accessible explanation. No essential service or contact information depends on the animation.

## Performance
Use one masked surface or a bounded render layer; avoid many worker DOM fragments and full-resolution duplicate scenes. Stop ambient work when invisible.

## Validation
Inspect contact paths, reach, silhouette, scale, reveal edges and pullback continuity at five progress values. A tiny illustrated diagram is not a photorealistic production asset.
