# Figure to garment and collection

Independent technical reconstruction from a locally inspected reference. Source media, platform identities, brands and frame captures remain private. Approximate times describe the recording, which may start mid-page or loop; they are not a specification of source code or easing.

## Creative objective
Let a selected item emerge from a wider collection through controlled fragmentation and scale.

## Visual description
A row of dressed figures opens into segmented panels, isolates one figure, then shows a garment and a larger product collection. A lifestyle image bridges catalogue regions.

## Temporal sequence and key moments
0–2s: figure row; 2–4s: fragmented panel transition; 4–7s: isolated figure and garment; 7–10s: catalogue/lifestyle passage; 10–13s: collection/hero return; remaining capture loops the sequence.

## Motion and camera
Panel masks and vertical displacement appear to reveal subject parts. This is not evidence of live cloth simulation or an articulated human rig.

## Implementation
Use a canonical subject image or licensed rig with consistent garment fit. If using strips, share a source coordinate system and converge all masks precisely before the detail state. Avoid detached limbs, changing body scale and stretching a person into clothing. A garment-only close view can replace unavailable anatomy.

## Interaction
Selectable figure cards lead to real product details. Scroll choreography may enrich the handoff but cannot replace size/price/detail controls with a recording.

## Responsive adaptation
Mobile shows one active subject with partial neighbours and a stable detail zone; preserve garment outline and accessible controls.

## Accessibility
Use a static figure/product pair for reduced motion. Supply meaningful product descriptions rather than relying on the model pose.

## Performance
Many duplicate high-resolution strip layers can multiply texture memory. Use few masks or a measured canvas pass and lazy-load noncritical lifestyle imagery.

## Validation
Check mask registration, garment identity, no cutout seams, direct product links, keyboard selection, resize and fast scroll.
