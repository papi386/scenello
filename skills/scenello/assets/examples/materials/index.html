01 / Mineral

Glazed ceramic

A hard surface with a fluid finish. Light collects at its curved edges, revealing the glaze's depth.

Properties & applications

Illustrated use: wall panels and tactile objects. This is an artistic material study, not a technical product specification.

02 / Metal

Brushed aluminium

Directional grain makes light a moving part of the surface. Quiet precision, without a mirror finish.

Properties & applications

Illustrated use: hardware and small architectural details. Colour and texture are symbolic rather than manufacturing references.

03 / Organic

Pressed cork

A granular surface that feels warm before it is touched. Irregularity becomes its own structure.

Properties & applications

Illustrated use: boards and soft furnishings. Source actual safety and performance data from a manufacturer before specifying a product.

04 / Mineral

Cast glass

Neither fully transparent nor opaque. Layers of colour gather where the material becomes thicker.

Properties & applications

Illustrated use: light diffusers and partitions. This original graphic does not represent a tested material.

05 / Metal

Folded copper

Folded planes alternate between warm highlight and deep shadow. Time will give the surface another voice.

Properties & applications

Illustrated use: sculptural detailing. The study focuses on light and form, not physical simulation.

06 / Organic

Structured paper

Small folds transform a delicate sheet into a landscape of ridges. Economy of means, richness of form.

Properties & applications

Illustrated use: packaging and temporary installations. Confirm fire, moisture and durability requirements for real applications.
