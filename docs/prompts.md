# Example requests

Use `$scenello` explicitly in Codex. Add your actual project constraints and assets.

1. **Landing page:** Build a cinematic page for a botanical archive. Compare three art directions, select one, keep specimens clickable and deliver browser evidence.
2. **Existing redesign:** Preserve this site's content and routes. Replace generic sections with a coherent editorial design; show the before/after at narrow and wide sizes.
3. **Hero:** Make human and mechanical fingertips meet on scroll, using normalized anchors. Investigate asset fidelity before selecting 2.5D or rigged 3D. Do not fake realistic anatomy with icons.
4. **Cards:** Let three project cards rotate around a diagonal axis and carry the lead directly into its detail view. Keep native scroll and keyboard access.
5. **Transition:** Connect this hero to its catalogue through one persistent focal object. Test fast scroll, reverse, resize and reduced motion.
6. **3D:** Add an inspectable product to this existing stack. Budget textures/draw calls, keep DOM controls, choose licensed assets and provide a static fallback.
7. **Responsive:** Preserve desktop's spatial idea but art-direct mobile. Keep text separate from imagery and test portrait, landscape and real touch when available.
8. **Design editing:** Remove repeated decorative cards, unnecessary buttons and vague copy without flattening the site's distinctive visual identity.
9. **Performance:** Investigate this scene's p95/p99 spikes. Separate main-thread work, rAF cadence, presentation and loading; retain raw traces and report failures.
10. **References:** Inspect this authorized local reference pack safely. Keep a private ledger, explain the useful mechanisms independently and create original SVG storyboards; publish no source frames.

Negative example: “Edit a 36-second promotional reel and choose its soundtrack” is outside Scenello's web-engineering scope.
